Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Loco Music Player (“Loco”) is developed by Primož Škrjanc. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Loco on Mac, iPad and iPhone.

Loco doesn’t collect any data

Loco doesn’t collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn’t contact any server operated by the developer or by third parties.

What stays on your device

To show your library, Loco reads the music files in the folders you choose and keeps a library database, your queue, playlists and settings on your device. This data never leaves your device through Loco. If you delete Loco, this data is deleted with it.

When Loco edits tags or lyrics, it writes only to the files you asked it to change.

iCloud Drive

If you add a folder from iCloud Drive, your device and Apple handle syncing and downloading those files, under Apple’s privacy policy. Loco doesn’t receive or send any data of its own.

Children

Loco doesn’t collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
